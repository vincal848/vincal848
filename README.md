### Hi, I'm Caleb Vinson

Master's student in **Computational Finance at Carnegie Mellon University**. I work on
algorithmic and systematic trading, derivatives pricing, and forecasting in commodity and
equity markets. Most of my work is statistical modeling and simulation in Python, R, and C++.

**Current work**
- Agentic power modeling of the NYISO region: interzonal spreads, LMP forecasting, DARTs, FTRs
- Reinforcement-learning market maker driven by multivariate Hawkes processes ([MarketMicrostructure](https://github.com/vincal848/MarketMicrostructure), with a C matching engine in [lob-engine-c](https://github.com/vincal848/lob-engine-c))
- IMC Prosperity trading competition
- Volatility and ETF trading algorithms
